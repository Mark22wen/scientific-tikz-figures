两个正值线性热阻并联连接理想恒温端点 Th 和 Tc，且 Th > Tc。稳态、无内热源且支路之间无侧向换热。每支路用总热阻 Ri（K/W）表征；热流率 Qdot_i 的单位为 W，不能与热流密度混用。箭头定义从热端到冷端的正方向，实心圆点表示连接，矩形为等效热阻。总热流率按守恒相加。图表达物理约束，不表达算法的并行计算或操作顺序。此为原创教学示意，无实验数据，非比例图。
Two positive linear thermal resistances connect ideal reservoirs at Th and Tc in parallel, with Th > Tc. Assume steady state, no internal heat generation, and no lateral heat exchange between branches. Ri is the total thermal resistance in K/W; the heat-transfer rate Qdot_i is in W and is distinct from heat flux density. Arrows define positive heat transfer from hot to cold, filled dots denote connections, and rectangles represent equivalent thermal resistances. Conservation gives the sum of the branch rates. This original teaching schematic describes physical constraints, not parallel algorithm execution or process order; it contains no experimental data and is not to scale.
